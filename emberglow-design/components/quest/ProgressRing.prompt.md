The focus-timer ember ring from the Active Quest screen.

```jsx
<ProgressRing progress={0.82} size={260}>
  <div style={{fontFamily:'var(--font-body)', fontWeight:700, fontSize:56, color:'var(--ember-bone)'}}>12:34</div>
  <EyebrowLabel tone="muted">Of 15:00</EyebrowLabel>
</ProgressRing>
```

Thin Cinnabar arc, glowing, rounded cap, starts at 12 o'clock. Also used as the loading indicator — never use spinners.
